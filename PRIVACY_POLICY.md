# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-07-10**

## Introduction

HanCast ("we", "our", or "us") is an open-source screen casting application that enables users to cast media files and URLs to devices on their local network. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

### Information We Do NOT Collect

HanCast is designed with privacy in mind. We do **not** collect, store, or transmit:

- Personal identification information
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Browsing history
- Media content or filenames
- Device information
- Network data beyond local network operation

### Local Operation

All HanCast functionality operates entirely on your local network:

- Device discovery uses SSDP/UPnP protocols within your LAN
- Media streaming occurs directly between your computer and target devices
- No data is sent to external servers

## How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no information to use, share, or sell.

## Third-Party Services

HanCast may interact with:

- **MPV Media Player**: Open-source media playback (bundled or external)
- **Local Network Devices**: DLNA/UPnP compatible devices on your network

These interactions occur entirely on your local network and do not involve external data transmission.

## Data Storage

HanCast stores only:

- Application settings (language, window preferences)
- Device names and preferences (stored locally in AppData)

This data remains on your device and is never transmitted elsewhere.

## Children's Privacy

HanCast does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
